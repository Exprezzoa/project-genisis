# 02 — World and Lore

> Public-facing lore is in §1–§6. The hidden truth is summarized in §7, with full detail in `04_campaign_and_secrets.md`. **Signage, logs, posters and broadcasts in the game must reflect the PUBLIC version** unless the clue map says a specific item leaks a truth.

## 1. The Age of Ash, 2187

- Humanity lives in **fortified settlements** separated by dangerous wilderness. The largest are linked by military-controlled **transport corridors** (rail lines with armored trains and Frame escorts).
- Beyond them: abandoned cities, ruined industry, dormant research stations, unmapped land.
- Most people have never been outside their settlement's protected zone.
- The era is called **the Age of Ash** because a dead Titan doesn't rot. It **calcifies and crumbles into grey ash**. Old battlefields are ash fields. Ash on the wind is normal weather.

## 2. Official history (what everyone believes, Elias included)

| Year | Official event |
|---|---|
| 2030s–2050s | **The Scorch.** Climate collapse, failed harvests, heat belts. Hard times, "but humanity endured." |
| 2067 | **The First Emergence.** Giant biological creatures, the **Titans**, appear across populated regions "without warning." Cities fall. Militaries collapse. |
| 2068 | Surviving governments unite into the **Unified Defense Directorate (UDD)**, usually called "the Directorate." |
| 2071 | The first **Frame**, the *Mk.0 Bulwark*, defeats a Titan. Frames are powered by a "synthetic **Reactive Core**." |
| 2070s–2100s | **The Settlement Era.** Bulwark walls, fortified settlements, the corridor network. |
| 2100s–now | **Reclamation.** Slow recovery of territory. Frame pilots are heroes. |

**Official conclusion:** humanity was nearly destroyed by the Titans, and only Directorate discipline prevented extinction. Schools teach a simple version. Broadcasts celebrate each extermination. Recruitment posters show smiling pilots in front of giant machines.

**Propaganda lines** (use on posters, PA and screens):
- "THE FRAME STANDS. HUMANITY STANDS."
- "DISCIPLINE IS SURVIVAL."
- "EVERY WALL WAS BUILT BY SOMEONE WHO CARED."
- "REPORT ANOMALIES. TRUST YOUR DIRECTORATE."
- "ASH IS A MEMORY. THE BULWARK IS A PROMISE."

## 4. The Directorate (UDD)

- **Structure:** Command Council → Divisions (Frame Corps, Frontier Defense, Corridor Security, Science Division) → units.
- **Frame Corps** fields squads of 2–4 Frames. Squads have callsigns. Elias and Mara form **Ember Squad**: Mara is **Ember-1** (lead, top of class), Elias is **Ember-2**.
- **Ranks used in-game:** Pilot Cadet → Pilot Officer → Lieutenant → Captain → Commander. Elias and Mara are newly commissioned **Pilot Officers**.
- **Halden Overwatch** is the operations radio net. Voice: Corporal Imani Okafor.
- **Insignia:** an amber chevron over a stylized wall (build it from 3 wedges plus a bar). Division colors: slate blue-grey `#4A5A6A` and amber `#E8A33D`.

## 5. Titans (public knowledge)

- Giant biological organisms, 30–80+ studs tall. They wander the wilderness and sometimes enter protected land.
- **Behavior is "not fully understood."** Some attack on sight, some ignore people, some get more aggressive near Frames, some seem to recognize particular machines. Officially these are "behavioral variance."
- **Classification:** by **Class** (size: Class-1 under 35 studs, Class-2 35–50, Class-3 50–70, Class-4 70+) and **type name** (body plan: Strider, Bulwark-back, Crawler, Siren, Dormant...).
- **They regenerate.** You can't chip them down forever. Pilots exploit species-specific weak points: tendon clusters, sensory crests, and the **Dorsal Core** of Strider-types.
- **Death:** calcifies, vents grey vapor, collapses into ash over ~12 seconds.
- **Feral Titans' glow** (eyes, veins, cores) is **orange-red** (`#FF7A2E` / `#E25B2D`).

## 6. Frames (public knowledge)

- Giant humanoid combat machines, 35–50 studs tall, piloted from a chest cockpit.
- Powered by a **Reactive Core** (publicly "synthetic"). The core makes Frames adaptive and fast. **Its glow is amber** (`#FFB347`).
- The Directorate controls all manufacturing, maintenance and access. Civilians never see core internals.
- Pilots need training because control blends mechanical input with a "neural response mechanism."
- "The pilot remains human. The machine remains a machine." (academy motto)
- **Classes:** Vanguard (balanced melee), Striker (mobility), Bastion (defense), Ranger (precision ranged), Prototype (experimental). Details in `09_frames.md`.

## 7. Hidden truth (summary; see `04_campaign_and_secrets.md`)

1. Titans come from **Project GENESIS** (2049–2061), a secret program by the **Meridian Accord** (6 governments plus Halvorsen Biotech, Castell Industrial and Aurel Dynamics). It aimed to engineer organisms that could heal a dying planet.
2. The organism is **the Lattice**, a self-organizing cellular network that spreads by spores and is guided by a **resonance** signal.
3. The first containment failure, the **Kestrel Event (2061)**, was buried. The First Emergence (2067) was its consequence, and governments used the chaos to erase evidence.
4. **Every Frame core is living Lattice tissue** cultivated from Titans. Titans that "recognize" Frames are sensing their own kin tissue.
5. **Resonants** are rare humans whose nervous systems harmonize with Lattice. They make the best pilots. Under certain conditions they can become **Shifters**. **Elias is a Resonant. So was his father.**
6. The Lattice has a built-in **~120-year maturation cycle**. **The Bloom** (a second, larger emergence) is approaching.
7. Competing interests keep the secret: officials who fear collapse, careerists, researchers who want to fix the mistake, and some who want to finish what GENESIS started.
8. **Cyan** (`#7FE3F0`) is the color of intelligent resonance: Shifters' glow, and Elias's core when it reacts.

## 8. Shifters and factions (public knowledge: almost none)

Publicly, intelligent Titans don't exist until the Chapter 3 Halden incident. Internally:
- **Shifters:** rare individuals who move between human and Titan form, keeping their mind and memory. Their glow is cyan.
- **Nobody expects them (user direction, 2026-10-10, D-045):** to the people of the Third Frontier, Shifters are not a real thing. The first time one appears it is a **reveal**, and the reaction is fear and disbelief. Before that moment no on-screen character speaks of Shifters as fact.
- **How a Shifter transforms (user direction, 2026-10-10, D-039):** the change is triggered by **pain the Shifter inflicts on themselves**. A **bolt of yellow lightning** comes down on them with a **crack of thunder**; when the flash clears, the Titan form stands there. Shown without blood or visible injury (a sharp, deliberate gesture and a wince). The yellow lightning belongs to the moment of transformation only; a Shifter's glow afterwards is still cyan.
- **The Remnants:** a loose network who believe humanity must learn the truth. Methods differ: communication, sabotage, and a militant minority.
- **The Ascendants:** a radical movement that sees the Lattice as the next stage of evolution. Some will sacrifice others.
- **The Unbound:** independents. Some want quiet lives, some revenge, some just to survive.
- No faction is purely good or purely evil. Individuals inside each disagree.

## 9. Everyday life details (use for environmental storytelling)

- Ration chits, rail passes, ash masks hanging by doors, water-reclaimer hum.
- Kids collect "core-chip" trading cards of famous Frames.
- Broadcast radio: *Halden Morning Line*, Directorate news, a call-in music hour.
- Frontier soldiers write their unit number on their boots in white paint.
- Pilots tap the cockpit hatch twice before boarding (superstition). Elias does this. Mara pretends not to.
- Common slang: **"ashed"** = killed or destroyed, **"wallside"** = safe inside the Bulwark, **"out past the posts"** = in the wilderness, **"core-sick"** = nausea after long piloting (an early symptom of resonance strain, though nobody knows that yet).
