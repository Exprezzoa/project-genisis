## 2. Rules of the Lattice (keep these consistent)

1. Lattice tissue **regenerates** quickly unless its **core node** is damaged. That's why Titans regenerate and why weak points exist: each species has a core node in a different place.
2. Lattice cells communicate via **resonance**, a bio-signal felt as pressure, ringing and memory flashes by Resonants.
3. **Frame cores are Lattice.** They're "tamed" by an inhibitor lattice and a neural interface. A core grown from a specific Titan's tissue "remembers" its source. Titans sense their kin cores, which is why some Titans react to particular Frames.
4. **Resonants** can feel cores and, at high exposure, communicate with Lattice. Prolonged contact causes **core-sickness** (nausea, ringing, vivid memories), then resonance events, then possible **shifting**.
5. **Shifters** keep their mind in both forms. Transforming costs enormous energy. They glow **cyan** (feral glow is **orange-red**).
6. **Death:** when the core node is destroyed, the tissue calcifies into ash.
7. **The Quiet Code** must be broadcast by a Resonant through a large network of cores. **Every Frame in the Directorate is an antenna.** Humanity's weapons are the only means to end the threat, and broadcasting the code would also shut the Frames down.

## 3. Campaign outline (3 acts, 12 chapters)

**Act I — The Human Shield** (Ch1–4)
- **Ch1 The Last Quiet Morning:** fully specified in `05_chapter1_script.md`.
- **Ch2 Classified:** Elias and Mara dig into the spindle fragment. Voss runs tests ("core resonance coefficient 3.4× baseline"). Vale restricts the investigation and reveals he served with Tobias. Mission: escort a corridor train; a Titan ignores the train and stalks Elias's Frame. Mara's brother Teo's old medical file is a side thread.
- **Ch3 The Second Signal:** **Public discovery.** The Watcher enters Halden's restricted district, avoids civilians, disables communications, and heads for the Science Division vault. Elias fights to slow it. Orin appears in human form afterward in the chaos and speaks to Elias for the first time. The Directorate publicly calls it "an unprecedented enemy assault."
- **Ch4 Records Depot 3:** an infiltration of a classified archive on the corridor. **Act I ends** with evidence that the First Emergence was preceded by experiments (a 2061 Kestrel incident memo).

**Act II — The False History** (Ch5–8)
- Settlements whose histories differ from official records. Intelligent Titans recorded for decades. Remnants, Ascendants and Unbound encounters.
- **Orin reveals Frames and Titans are the same phenomenon.** Elias rejects it, suspecting manipulated evidence, until Voss confirms it from core biopsies.
- Rhein moves to stop the discoveries. Vale must choose between orders and his pilots. Mara loses faith in the military but disagrees with Elias on how to release the truth (she wants it done through official channels with proof; he wants to protect people first).
- **The Prototype Frame "PF-0 Herald"** is introduced. **Its core was grown from Tobias's tissue.**
- **Act II ends:** discovery of Site Kestrel. Records prove the pre-Emergence experiments, but not the full origin.

**Act III — The Open World** (Ch9–12)
- The truth leaks. Settlements destabilize. Civilians split between "dismantle the Frames" and "we'll be defenseless."
- Shifter factions clash over whether humans keep Lattice technology.
- Rhein launches **Operation Threshold** to seize Kestrel and control the Bloom. Orin believes it will trigger it.
- Elias works with people whose motives conflict. Tobias is found: alive, partly shifted, lucid in fragments.
- The final conflict decides what humanity becomes after learning the truth.
