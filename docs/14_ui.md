# 14 — UI Specification

**All UI is built in code** by client controllers using `Shared/Config/UIStyle.lua` and reusable components in `Client/UI/Components`. There are no images: only Frames, UIStroke, UICorner, UIGradient, UIListLayout/UIPadding, TextLabels and TextButtons. **Every screen must work on PC, gamepad (with selection focus via `GuiService.SelectedObject`) and phone.**

### 1.1 Angular style (user direction, 2026-10-10; D-031)

The UI takes its attitude from kinetic, angular menu design (the user named the Persona games as inspiration): bold flat shapes, leaning edges, tilted type, hard contrast, things that snap in. It is **inspiration only**: no layouts, fonts, logos or assets are copied, and the palette stays this game's (amber = Directorate, never red-on-black). Everything is still Frames and text.

- **Slab:** the basic surface is a parallelogram (edges lean 14°). It is built from three Frames: two end caps exactly as wide as the lean, each cut corner to corner by a two-step transparency `UIGradient`, and a plain middle. (One gradient can't cut a wide frame: Roblox scales a rotated gradient to the frame's short side.) Very thin bars (sliders) and layout containers stay rectangular.
- **Button:** paper-white slab, ink Michroma text. Hover or gamepad selection: the slab turns amber, the text nudges 6 px, a paper-white shadow slab appears offset by `Shadow`. "On" state (a toggle that is on, the current tab): ink slab, amber text, amber underline. Disabled: ghosted.
- **Title:** tilted (−4°) slab with a paper shadow: amber with ink text on dark backgrounds, ink with paper text on the amber band.
- **Menu screens:** the game view dimmed with ink at 35% transparency, crossed by a diagonal amber band on the left (it sweeps in when a screen opens) with a thin ink band beside it. The pause column sits on the band, tilted −4°.
- **HUD:** the objective is a tilted amber "OBJECTIVE" tag over the text on an ink bar; toasts are ink slabs with an amber underline whose text pops in; prompts are an ink slab with a tilted amber key box.
- **Readability rules stay:** subtitles are plain text with a stroke; body text is never tilted; minimum text size 12.

### 1.2 Character stage (user direction, 2026-10-10; D-032, D-033)

Every menu screen sits on one shared stage (`Client/UI/CharacterStage`), so the character is always there:

- **Left:** the diagonal amber band with the pause column.
- **Right:** a **one-color silhouette of the player's character** (ink) in front of a slanted color field with an accent stripe; a paper-white copy offset (14, 10) px is its shadow. Built without images: a clone of `StarterCharacter` in a ViewportFrame, every part white under pure ambient light, tinted with `ImageColor3`.
- **The silhouette moves.** It is posed by code every frame (`Client/UI/SilhouetteRig`, forward kinematics on the R15 joints; no uploaded animations). Three layers: the screen's **pose**, an **idle** layer on every screen (breathing, a slow look around, shoulders and arms swaying, a small bob), and the screen's own **gesture**. It runs only while a menu is open.
- **Each screen has its own shot, pose and colors.** Changing screens flies the camera there (0.55 s, `Quint Out`) while the body moves into the next pose (eased, rate 7/s), blends the field color and kicks the silhouette 36 px sideways. Opening the menu slides the band in from the left and the silhouette in from the right.
- **One color means only the outline reads:** every pose keeps a limb clear of the body, and shots stay wide enough to show it (close-ups turn into a black block).

| Screen | Shot | Pose and gesture | Field / stripe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pause | Full body, three-quarter view | Mid-stride, right fist held in front; the fist arm flexes | `AMBER` / `WARNING_ORANGE` |
| Settings | Waist up, from the front | Left hand raised to the side of the mask, elbow out; the hand adjusts it | `GLASS` blue-grey / `AMBER` |
| Mission Log | Waist up, from his right side | Right forearm raised, head bowed to read it; the wrist turns | `AMBER_LAMP` / `AMBER` |
| Equipment (Phase 6) | The raised forearm (to be tuned when the screen exists) | to be chosen | to be chosen |

New screens add one row to `CharacterStage.SHOTS` (`from`, `at`, `fov`, `field`, `stripe`, `pose`, `motion`). The shot should fit the screen's subject (the user's rule: "the setting fits the transition"). Screen content keeps to the left ~60% so the silhouette stays visible.

**Scaling:** each ScreenGui has a `UIScale` = clamp(min(viewportX / 1920, viewportY / 1080) × 1.0, 0.65, 1.25). On phones (smallest viewport side < 500 px) the scale is multiplied by 1.15 for readability. Every TextLabel has a `UITextSizeConstraint` with MinTextSize 12.

**ScreenGui order (DisplayOrder):** HUD 10, Prompts 15, Dialogue 20, Cards 30, Log/Menu 40, Cinematic letterbox 50, Fade 90, Loading 100. `ResetOnSpawn = false`, `IgnoreGuiInset = true` for full-screen layers, `ScreenInsets = CoreUISafeInsets` for HUD layers.

**Opening the menu is a small show** (user direction, D-034; the stage's own take on a "dive", nothing copied): three diagonal bars (paper, amber, ink) wipe across the screen from the right (0.5 s, 0.07 s apart); the dim fades in; the band slides in from the left; the camera starts 3.2 studs closer, 1.1 lower and 16° wider and pulls back to the shot (0.8 s, `Quint Out`); the character starts 1.6 studs low in a crouch and rises into the screen's pose; the field's motes rush upward (9× speed, dying down). Closing takes 0.16 s: the band and the silhouette leave the way they came.

**Field details** (`Client/UI/StageDressing`): 18 tilted squares drifting up; a thin line travelling down every 5 s; the screen's name in huge faint letters behind the silhouette (Michroma 120, 87% transparent; `PAUSED`, `SETTINGS`, `LOG`); and an ink readout tag in the bottom-right corner with a blinking amber diamond: `<ZONE>  //  T+hh:mm:ss` (the zone the player is in, the server's running time).

### 1.3 Motion and detail rules (user direction, 2026-10-10; D-034)

The user's bar for every UI, not only the menus: nothing just appears, and every surface carries a small detail.

- **Screens:** opening a screen runs its content in (`Components.cascade`): each title and button slides in 90 px from the left and fades up, 0.045 s apart (the whole run is capped at 0.5 s); large surfaces (`Body`, `Page`) slide up 70 px (`Transitions.slideIn`).
- **Buttons:** highlighted buttons show a small ink diamond sliding in at the left; pressing kicks the slab onto its shadow; list buttons can carry a mono index tag (`01`).
- **Wipes:** `Transitions.wipe(parent)` is the shared screen change (menu opening, loading screen exit).
- **HUD:** a new objective runs its tag in and types its text out (0.02 s per character, at most 0.6 s); a toast's underline draws itself from the left, it carries an amber diamond, and it fades out over 0.25 s; a subtitle line fades in while rising 10 px (0.18 s).
- **Prompts:** pop in from 70% scale with a small overshoot (0.22 s); the key box rocks slowly.
- Every new screen or HUD element (Frame HUD, dialogue, cards) is built to this bar from the start. Reduce Flashing (§12) still applies: none of these flash.

## 2. Loading screen (`ReplicatedFirst/LoadingScreen.client.lua`)
- Remove the default loading screen. Black `PanelSolid`. Centered "PROJECT GENESIS" (Michroma 40, letter-spaced) plus a rotating tip (TitilliumWeb 18, secondary) every 4 s plus a thin amber progress line (indeterminate sweep).
- Angular look (D-034), all plain Frames because it runs before `Components` has replicated: the title sits in ink on a tilted paper slab over an amber shadow; a diagonal amber band on the left; 14 drifting motes; a mono status tag in the bottom-right corner with a blinking diamond (`LOADING  //  ASSETS`, then `LOADING  //  COMMAND LINK`, then `READY`); the tip cross-fades; on exit three bars wipe across and the screen clears.

- Hide when `game:IsLoaded()` **and** the server sends `Server_Ready` (timeout 20 s → hide anyway).

## 5. Frame HUD (`FrameHUDController`)

### 5.1 Layout

**As built (D-051), in the angular style of §1.1:** the integrity bar is 10 leaning segments under a tilted amber `INTEGRITY` tag, with a thin rule below it ticked at 15%, 34% and 67%; the energy bar is 4 leaning segments. Ability slots are leaning ink slabs **92 × 72** (a 72-wide leaning slab was too narrow for `SPECIAL`): key glyph top-left, name at the bottom, energy pips top-right, a dark sheet and a seconds readout while cooling down; the slot of the move being performed turns amber and kicks onto a paper shadow; unavailable slots are dimmed. The banner is an ink slab that drops in while its amber underline draws. On boarding, the blocks slide in 0.05 s apart. A refused action answers on the HUD: no energy flashes the energy bar (once with Reduce Flashing, else twice), a cooldown makes the slot twitch, locked weapons re-show the banner. The boot overlay is not built yet (Phase 5). **Titan bar, Core Strike prompt, lock-on marker and damage vignette are built (D-059, D-060; `TitanHUDController`, `LockOnController`):** the health bar adds a pale ghost of what the last hit took; a hint line under the bar gives the lock-on keys (`[R] LOCK ON`, then `LOCKED: <zone>  [C] ZONE  [R] RELEASE`); the bar kicks sideways when the Titan is hit and drops in and out from above.

### 5.2 Lock-on marker
BillboardGui on the zone's hurtbox (AlwaysOnTop, 64 × 64): a diamond made of 4 amber bars rotated 45°, slowly rotating (90°/s), plus a zone label under it (Michroma 11: `BODY`, `ANKLE L`, `ANKLE R`, `HEAD`, `CORE`). The Core marker uses `TitanHP` color and pulses.

### 5.3 Cockpit boot and anomaly effects
- **Boot overlay** (`05_chapter1_script.md` S08): centered RobotoMono 18 amber, typed at 40 chars/s, each line fading in. The total sequence is 2.5 s, then HUD elements slide into place (stagger 0.05 s).
- **Anomaly glitch** (S33): (1) a jitter loop: every 0.05 s, each HUD element gets a random offset of ±4 px and transparency flicker 0–0.3; (2) the system panel prints the anomaly lines; (3) the core indicator (a small 12 × 12 square in the system panel) and the integrity bar fill tween to `Resonance` cyan; (4) shutdown: every element tweens `Size.Y` → 0 over 0.3 s, then a single centered amber line `CORE OFFLINE`, then black.

## 7. Mission Log (Tab) and Log Reader
- Full-screen overlay (`Panel` at 0.1 transparency, blur via `BlurEffect` Size 12 while open).
- **Tabs** (Michroma 14): `OBJECTIVES` | `EVIDENCE` | `PEOPLE`.
  - **OBJECTIVES:** the current objective (large) plus completed objectives of the chapter (secondary, with a ✓ made of 2 rotated bars).
  - **EVIDENCE:** a list on the left (titles, e.g. "Outpost 07 Command Log", with "NEW" chips) and the reader on the right.
  - **PEOPLE:** short bios that update with the story (text only, no meters): Elias, Mara, Vale, Okafor, Hollis, Voss, and Rhein (after S40).
- **Log Reader** (also opened directly from evidence prompts): a modal 760 × 520 terminal: `PanelSolid`, amber RobotoMono 16 text with line spacing 1.25, a header bar with the log title, scanlines (a UIGradient with repeated transparency bands, 3% opacity), and a close button (glyph: E/B/✕).

## 8. Menu and settings
- **Menu:** Resume, Mission Log, Settings, Restart from Checkpoint (missions only, with a confirm dialog), Quit to Hub (disabled in the Vertical Slice).
- **Settings** (each is a row with a label on the left and a control on the right; gamepad-navigable):
  - Camera Sensitivity (slider 0.2–2.0, step 0.05), Invert Y (toggle), Camera Shake (0–100%), Sprint/Stride Mode (Hold/Toggle), Subtitles (On/Off), Subtitle Size (S/M/L), Music Volume, SFX Volume, Ambience Volume, Reduce Flashing (toggle), Tutorial Hints (toggle), Damage Numbers (toggle).
  - Changes apply immediately and are sent via `Settings_Update` (debounced 1.0 s).

## 10. Tutorial card
Top center (0.5, 0, 0, 120), width 560: title (Michroma 14, amber) + body (TitilliumWeb 18) with device-specific glyph text highlighted in amber. Slide down plus fade. Auto-hide after 5 s or on tap.

## 11. Fade layer
A single full-screen black Frame at DisplayOrder 90. `Fade(to, duration)` tweens its transparency. It's the **only** fade used by all systems (no duplicate fade frames).

## 12. Reduce flashing
When enabled: no Titan hit-flash Highlight, damage vignette max opacity 0.25, resonance ping and anomaly white-out capped at 30% intensity, and the core-pulse amplitude halved.
